Our year

Annual calendar

Most of our events happen at the same time each year, so schools and sponsors can plan ahead.

  1. February

    Competition season

    School teams compete in Ghana's robotics competitions, such as AIRTAD.

  2. April

    VEX Robotics World Championship

    Qualifying Ghanaian teams compete with the world's best. 2026 was held in St. Louis, Missouri.

  3. School holidays

    Future Engineers BootCamp

    Engineering challenges and the Ultimate Robot Challenge.

  4. October

    Girl Powered STEM Workshop

    Held around the International Day of the Girl Child.

  5. December

    VEX Robotics Ghana National Championship

    Top teams qualify for the next World Championship.
