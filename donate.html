Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, for US taxpayers. Bountiful STEM Educational Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 87-2018269). Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and we send a receipt for every donation.

Where does my money go?

Your gift funds robotics kits, teacher training, competition registration and travel, and the running of our STEM centers in Ghana.

Can I give every month?

Yes. Monthly gifts let us plan programs a full year ahead. Choose the monthly option when you give online, or contact us to set one up.

Does my employer match gifts?

Many companies match employee donations. Ask your HR team and give them our name and EIN.

Can I donate used computers or tablets?

Yes. Working laptops, desktops and tablets are welcome. Email us with the type and number of devices and we will arrange collection.

Can I volunteer from outside Ghana?

Yes. Engineers, teachers and STEM professionals can mentor students and teachers online, and some volunteers join us in person for workshops and competitions.
