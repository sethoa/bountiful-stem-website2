STEM, robotics & AI education in Ghana

Ghana's future engineers start here.

We give children in rural and underserved communities equal access to robotics, coding, artificial intelligence and computer science, and train the teachers who keep it going.

Donate today Explore our programs

Our mission: transforming lives through equitable access to STEM, robotics, AI, coding and computer science education.

Our programs

Programs that turn curiosity into skills

Every program is hands-on. Students build, code and test real machines, and teachers leave able to run the lessons on their own.

School STEM & Robotics Clubs

Weekly after-school clubs where students design, build and code robots with VEX IQ and VEX V5 kits, led by teachers we train.

Learn more →

Teacher Training

Hands-on professional development so rural teachers can teach robotics, coding and science with confidence.

Learn more →

Girl Powered STEM Workshop

Our annual workshop opening robotics, AI, VR and IoT to girls, with mentoring from women in STEM careers.

Learn more →

Future Engineers BootCamp

A holiday bootcamp of real engineering challenges that ends with the Ultimate Robot Challenge.

Learn more →

Robotics Competitions

We prepare and sponsor teams from school events to the Ghana National Championship and VEX Worlds.

Learn more →

STEM Centers & Equipment

Community STEM centers and donated kits and computers that turn ordinary classrooms into labs.

Learn more →
