Who we are

Closing the STEM gap between Ghana's cities and its villages

Bountiful STEM Educational Foundation is a US-based 501(c)(3) global nonprofit transforming lives through equitable access to STEM, robotics, artificial intelligence, coding and computer science education.

Mission

To transform lives through equitable access to STEM, robotics, AI, coding and computer science education, starting with the rural and underserved communities that need it most.

Vision

A Ghana and West Africa where every rural community has trained STEM teachers and a STEM center its children can walk to.

Our story

It started with one district and a box of robots

Bountiful STEM Educational Foundation was founded in Ghana in 2019 by Bountiful Technologies Ltd, and was established as a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Utah in 2021.

In late 2019, our team ran its first teacher-training sessions in Abomosu, in Ghana's Atiwa West District. Teachers were eager, but their schools had no equipment to practice with. In August 2020, with help from supporters in the United States, including former HP computers executive Chet Harmer and two retired American educators, we donated robotics kits worth more than GH₵60,000 to the district. Schools used them to launch STEM clubs across six circuits.

That model guides us today: train local teachers, equip their schools, and support students from their first robot all the way to international competition.

What we believe

  • Access for all. Where a child lives should not decide what they can learn.
  • Learning by doing. Students learn engineering by building, failing, fixing and building again.
  • Girls in STEM. We work for equal representation of girls in every club, workshop and team.
  • Lasting change. We train local teachers so programs keep running long after our visit.

Milestones

Our journey so far

Each step built on the last: training came first, then equipment, then clubs, then competition.

Our founder's work was featured in BYU Magazine's “Putting Down STEM Roots in Ghana.” Read the article.

  1. 2019

    Founded in Ghana

    First teacher training in Abomosu, Atiwa West District, in December 2019 and January 2020.

  2. August 2020

    GH₵60,000+ in robotics kits

    Kits donated to Atiwa West launch school STEM clubs across six circuits.

  3. 2021

    US 501(c)(3) in Utah

    The foundation is established in Washington, Utah, so US supporters can give tax-deductibly.

  4. 2022

    Ghana's first VEX IQ teams at Worlds

    Elementary and middle school students represent Ghana at the VEX Robotics World Championship in Dallas.

  5. April 2024

    18 students to Worlds

    Students from schools across Ghana compete in Dallas for the third straight year.

  6. October 2024

    Girl Powered 5.0

    More than 150 students explore robotics, coding, AI, VR and IoT in Accra.

  7. January 2025

    Rural STEAM center

    With Dreamers Wish Foundation, we launch a center for robotics, coding, AR/VR, AI, electronics and vocational skills.

  8. February 2026

    AIRTAD'26 champions

    Labone SHS, a team we sponsor, wins the AIRTAD'26 senior high robotics competition.
