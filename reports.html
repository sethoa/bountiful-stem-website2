Reports & financials

Accountable to every donor

We are a registered US 501(c)(3) public charity. Here is where to find our filings and how to request our reports.

501(c)(3) status

Bountiful STEM Educational Foundation is a tax-exempt public charity under section 501(c)(3) of the US Internal Revenue Code. EIN 87-2018269.

Public filings

Our IRS filings and nonprofit profile are publicly available through independent charity databases.

Charity Navigator profile ↗

Annual reports

Our annual reports describe the students reached, teachers trained and how every dollar was spent. Email us to request the latest copy.

Request a report →

Where your money goes

Your gift pays for the things that put STEM in children's hands: robotics kits and spare parts, teacher training, competition registration and travel, and the running of our STEM centers in Ghana.

Tax receipts

Every donor receives a receipt for their records. Gifts from US taxpayers are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Contact us if you need a duplicate receipt or a year-end statement.
