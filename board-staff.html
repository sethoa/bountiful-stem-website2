Board & staff

The people behind Bountiful STEM

Our founders, board and team in Ghana and the United States share one goal: equal access to STEM for every child.

Leadership

Founders

SA

Seth Ogoe Ayim, PMP

Co-founder & Executive Director

Seth leads our programs in Ghana. He is also co-founder and CEO of Bountiful Technologies Ltd, an education technology company supporting K-12 STEM, AI, robotics, IoT, VR and computer science across Ghana and West Africa. He led Ghana's first elementary and middle school VEX IQ teams to the World Championship and spoke on STEM in Ghanaian classrooms at the 2024 VEX Robotics Educators Conference.

Tom Campbell

Co-founder & President

Tom has served as co-founder and president of Bountiful STEM Educational Foundation since 2019, guiding the foundation's work in the United States and its partnerships with donors and schools.
