Get involved

There's a place for everyone at Bountiful STEM

Volunteer your skills, bring your company on board, run a fundraiser or donate equipment. Every way you help reaches a real classroom in Ghana.

Volunteer

Share your skills with young engineers

Engineers, teachers, university students and STEM professionals help our students and teachers in person in Ghana and online from anywhere.

Sign up to volunteer
  • Online mentor. Coach a robotics team or a teacher over video calls.
  • Competition judge or referee. Help run the Ghana National Championship and local events.
  • Workshop volunteer. Lead a session at Girl Powered or the Future Engineers BootCamp.
  • Career speaker. Tell students how you got into STEM.
  • Skilled volunteer. Help with design, video, grant writing or fundraising.

Corporate partners

Partner with us to grow Ghana's STEM talent

Companies and foundations help us train teachers, equip schools and send teams to compete. We report back on the results of every partnership.

Become a partner
  • Sponsor a school club for a year of kits, training and coaching.
  • Sponsor a team on its journey to the VEX Robotics World Championship.
  • Sponsor an event such as Girl Powered or the National Championship.
  • Employee volunteering as mentors, judges and speakers.
  • In-kind gifts of computers, software and robotics equipment.

Plan a fundraiser

Ideas for creating your own fundraiser

Rally your friends, school or workplace. Tell us your plan and we'll send photos, stories and a donation link to share.

Birthday fundraiser

Ask friends to give to Bountiful STEM instead of gifts, on Facebook, LinkedIn or by email.

School robotics drive

Students in the US raise money so students in Ghana can build their first robots.

Workplace giving

Run a giving week at work and ask your employer to match what your team raises.

Run, walk or demo day

Host a fun run, a robotics demo or a community dinner with a short program about our students.

Donate equipment

Your old laptop can become a coding lab

We accept working laptops, desktops and tablets, plus robotics kits, sensors and electronics components. Tell us what you have and we'll arrange collection and shipping to Ghana.

Offer equipment
  • Laptops and desktops less than about 8 years old, in working order
  • Tablets with chargers
  • VEX IQ and VEX V5 kits, parts and controllers
  • Sensors, microcontrollers and electronics kits
