Programs

Hands-on STEM, from a child's first robot to the world stage

Six connected programs train teachers, equip schools and give students ages 5–18 a clear path from curiosity to competition.

School STEM & Robotics Clubs

After-school clubs in rural schools where students design, build and code robots using VEX IQ and VEX V5 systems. Clubs meet weekly and are led by teachers we have trained.

  • Robotics kits and curriculum provided
  • Coding, design thinking and teamwork
  • Clubs feed into local and national competitions

Teacher Professional Development

Practical training that equips teachers to teach robotics, coding and science through projects. Our first cohort trained in Abomosu, Atiwa West District, in 2019–2020.

  • Hands-on workshops, not lectures
  • Ready-to-use lesson plans
  • Ongoing coaching after training

Girl Powered STEM Workshop

An annual workshop for students ages 5–18 with a focus on girls, held around the International Day of the Girl Child. Participants try robotics, coding, artificial intelligence, virtual reality and the Internet of Things, then meet women working in STEM careers.

  • Six editions held so far
  • 150+ participants at the 2024 edition
  • Supported by VEX Robotics and the REC Foundation

Future Engineers BootCamp

A holiday bootcamp where students take on real engineering challenges, such as designing earthquake-resistant structures, and finish with the Ultimate Robot Challenge competition.

  • Project-based engineering design
  • Team challenges and a final competition
  • 2026 registration is open

Robotics Competitions

We prepare and sponsor teams for the VEX IQ Robotics Competition (elementary and middle school) and the VEX V5 Robotics Competition (high school), from school events to the Ghana National Championship and beyond.

  • Ghanaian teams at VEX Worlds in 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2026
  • Labone SHS, a team we sponsor, won AIRTAD'26
  • Travel support for students who could not otherwise go

STEM Centers & Equipment

We set up community STEM centers and equip schools with robotics kits, computers and learning software. Our STEM center is in Weija, Accra, and in 2025 we opened a rural STEAM center with Dreamers Wish Foundation.

  • Robotics, coding, AR/VR, AI and electronics
  • Donated computers become coding labs
  • See our locations

For schools and districts

How a school joins the program

  1. Reach out

    A head teacher or district office contacts us with the school's location and number of students.

  2. Train teachers

    Two or more teachers complete our hands-on robotics and coding training.

  3. Launch the club

    We provide kits and curriculum, and the school starts weekly STEM club sessions.

  4. Compete and grow

    Students enter competitions, and strong clubs become hubs for nearby schools.
